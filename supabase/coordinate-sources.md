# Destination coordinate sources

These coordinates populate the existing `tourism_sites.latitude` and `tourism_sites.longitude` fields. They are map pins, not surveyed site boundaries.

| Destination | Latitude | Longitude | Interpretation and source |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Timoga Cold Springs | 8.191261 | 124.179791 | Representative reference point at Timoga Spring Pool within the multi-resort Timoga springs area. [Lakad Pilipinas](https://www.lakadpilipinas.com/2013/10/timoga-springs-iligan-city.html) publishes this GPS point. |
| Sikyop Agri-Tourism Adventure | 8.24705 | 124.42295 | Approximate study-area location. Converted from the paper's 8°14.8230′ N, 124°25.3770′ E for Sikyop Agri-tourism Adventure Park in Rogongon. [Davao Research Journal study](https://www.davaoresearchjournal.ph/index.php/main/article/download/479/252). |

Timoga Cold Springs covers several independently operated pools, so its point identifies a reference attraction rather than the extent of the whole area. The Sikyop coordinate is approximate research-site data rather than a surveyed entrance. Confirm both pins with the destination managers before using them for emergency access, boundary drawing, or turn-by-turn routing.
