# IliGuide SRS alignment summary

The completed SRS retains the 27-section Volere Version 16 sequence, original authors, CCC181 course and IS3A.1 section. It documents the four roles—visitor, Gatekeeper, Tourism Office Admin, and Site Admin—and separates public destination access from staff permissions scoped by management organization and assigned destination.

Functional scope aligns to the repository’s visitor discovery and reservations, visitor records, QR ticketing and attendance, walk-ins, schedules, capacity displays, announcements and notifications, feedback, demo payment review, and staff reporting. The SRS distinguishes expected reservations from confirmed attendance; current occupancy from approved limits, booking capacity, and physical estimates; and simulated GCash/Maya workflows from real payment settlement.

The document includes 28 identified functional requirements with fit criteria and links to goals, use cases, and system areas. It also includes a 12-item quality acceptance register. Proposed targets are labeled as proposals, not stakeholder approvals or measured results. Current repository code and migrations inform implementation alignment; they do not establish deployment, production configuration, stakeholder acceptance, or successful independent testing.

Appendix C preserves four relevant original as-is process diagrams with captions and text descriptions. Irrelevant Volere examples and generic road-scheduling diagrams are excluded. Companion files provide the alignment report, discrepancy register, verified bibliography, and FR traceability matrix.
